July 2026

Reset Event OVERRIDE

Pren (the host of July's event) lost access to the world where the event was taking place (the judges sending it to the void). As such, he had to improvise, and decided to send all players through 6 worlds that were not intended for use yet. Items are earned back by completing (leaving) the worlds.

Main universes reorganized - split back into multiverse, each centered around one of the six True Colors, including:

  • Red: Destiny
  • Orange: Purpose
  • Yellow: Connection
  • Green: Memory
  • Blue: Madness
  • Purple: Tragedy
